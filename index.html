Privacy Policy

Net Worth Explained publishing tool (Meta app "video-engine"). Last updated 31 October 2026.

This app is a private tool used only by the owner of the Net Worth Explained channel to publish the channel's own videos as Reels to its own Instagram account (@networthexplainedofficial). It is not offered to the public and no other person can sign in to it.

What the app accesses

When the owner connects the channel's Instagram account through Facebook Login, the app receives access tokens and the IDs and names of the owner's Facebook Page and linked Instagram professional account. It uses them to publish the channel's own videos, attach a sound from Instagram's licensed audio library, and read view counts for those Reels.

What the app does not do

It does not collect, read, store or share data about any other Instagram or Facebook user. It does not read messages, comments or followers. It does not sell data, show ads or share data with third parties.

Where data is kept

Tokens and account IDs are stored only on the owner's own computer, in a private file that is never published. They are used only to call Meta's APIs.

Data deletion

The only data the app holds is the owner's own connection. To delete it, the owner presses Disconnect on the channel's card in the app, which deletes the stored tokens and account IDs, and can also remove the app under Facebook Settings, Apps and websites. Anyone who believes the app holds data about them can email kapil@cotravel.ca and it will be deleted within 30 days.
