Privacy Policy

Net Worth Explained publishing tool (Meta app and Google app "video-engine"). Last updated 4 November 2026.

This app is a private tool used only by the owner of the Net Worth Explained channel to publish the channel's own videos to its own YouTube channel and as Reels to its own Instagram account (@networthexplainedofficial). It is not offered to the public and no other person can sign in to it.

What the app accesses

When the owner connects the channel's Instagram account through Facebook Login, the app receives access tokens and the IDs and names of the owner's Facebook Page and linked Instagram professional account. It uses them to publish the channel's own videos, attach a sound from Instagram's licensed audio library, and read view counts for those Reels.

YouTube and Google data

When the owner connects the channel's YouTube account through Google Sign-In, the app asks for three permissions: to upload videos (youtube.upload), to manage the channel's own videos, such as setting a thumbnail and publish time (youtube.force-ssl), and to read the channel's own YouTube Analytics (yt-analytics.readonly). It receives a sign-in token and the channel's ID and title. It uses them only to upload and schedule the channel's own videos and Shorts and to read view counts and watch time for those videos, shown to the owner on a private dashboard.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not used for advertising, not sold, not shared with third parties and not read by people other than the owner.

What the app does not do

It does not collect, read, store or share data about any other YouTube, Google, Instagram or Facebook user. It does not read messages, comments, subscribers or followers. It does not sell data, show ads or share data with third parties.

Where data is kept

Tokens and account IDs are stored only on computers the owner controls, in private files that are never published. They are used only to call Google's and Meta's APIs.

Data deletion

The only data the app holds is the owner's own connection. To delete it, the owner presses Disconnect on the channel's card in the app, which deletes the stored tokens and account IDs. Access can also be removed at any time under Google Account, Security, Third-party apps with account access, and under Facebook Settings, Apps and websites. Anyone who believes the app holds data about them can email kapil@cotravel.ca and it will be deleted within 30 days.
